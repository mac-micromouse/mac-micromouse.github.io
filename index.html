About

Originally started by IEEE in the late 1970s, Micromouse is a robotics competition where tiny robots race through a maze completely on their own. Teams design, build, and program their robots to explore the maze and find the fastest route to the center as quickly as possible.

McMaster Micromouse hosts workshops and help-sessions throughout the year leading to an end-of-year competition, giving students hands-on experience across embedded systems, PCB design, control theory, and pathfinding algorithms.

All levels of experience are welcomed!

Meet the Team

What You'll Learn

Embedded Systems

Write firmware for microcontrollers, work with sensors, and implement real-time control loops.

Hardware Design

Design and assemble custom circuits, motor drivers, and power systems for a compact robot.

Algorithms

Implement maze-solving algorithms like flood fill and A* to find the optimal path to the center.

Mechanical

Design chassis, choose drive configurations, and tune your robot's physical performance.
